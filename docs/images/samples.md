# Sample plans

Device Floorplan comes with seven sample plans. Pick one when you create a plan to try everything out; replace it later with your own image (**⋮ → Replace image**), and all positions are kept.

All samples are top-down, with a transparent background, so they look good in light and dark themes.

| Sample | Good for |
|---|---|
| Studio (open plan) | One open room plus bathroom |
| One-bedroom apartment | Small flats |
| Two-bedroom apartment | Flats with a balcony |
| Two-bedroom house with garden | Houses; the garden stays visible |
| House – ground floor | Two-floor demo, together with… |
| House – upper floor (3 bedrooms) | …the ground floor (same house) |
| Basement / two-car garage | Garages and basements |

![The sample plans in the image chooser](/screens/image-chooser.webp){.shot}

The files live in `custom_components/device_floorplan/frontend/templates/`, described in `templates.json`.
