# 1 · Measure

Measuring is optional. It gives the plan a real scale, so the scale bar, distances and the exported positions in metres are correct. Placing devices works without it.

## Draw a measurement

1. Click the start of something you know the length of: a wall, a door, a room.
2. Move the mouse and click the end. Lines snap straight when they are nearly horizontal or vertical (hold **Shift** to stop that).
3. Type the real length in metres and press **Set scale** (or Enter). For the second direction the field already holds an **estimate** from the first measurement: type the real length over it, or keep it.

Changed your mind? **✕** in the orange box or **Esc** discards the line you're drawing. Clicking a saved line's label always opens it for editing, even while you're drawing a new one.

![Typing the real length](/screens/measure-length.webp){.shot}

For the best accuracy, draw **one horizontal and one vertical** measurement. Use long walls: the longer the line, the smaller the error.

## Change or delete a measurement

![Editing a measurement](/screens/measure-edit.webp){.shot}

- **Drag the round ends** of a line to adjust it.
- **Drag the label** to move the whole line.
- **Click the label** to change the length or delete it (🗑).
- The **Measurements** list on the right has ✎ and 🗑 for each direction.
- Select a line and press **Delete** to remove it.

Drawing a new line in the same direction replaces the old one; a message with **Undo** appears.

## Horizontal and vertical disagree

If the two measurements give scales that differ by more than 5 %, the list shows a warning. Usually one line is slightly off: check both. If the image really is stretched (common with AI-generated or scanned plans), Device Floorplan simply uses a separate horizontal and vertical scale.

![Both directions measured](/screens/measure-done.webp){.shot}

## Angled images

For an *Angled view* image, measuring is turned off: perspective makes distances meaningless.
