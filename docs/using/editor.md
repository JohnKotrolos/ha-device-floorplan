# The editor

The editor has a header with your floors, the three steps, a guide box, the plan, and a list on the right. It always fits the window: the plan grows or shrinks to use the space that is left, and the legend under it always stays visible.

![The editor while placing devices](/screens/place-auto.webp){.shot}

## Header

- **Floor tabs**, sorted by level. The **⋮** inside a tab opens that floor's menu (settings, rename, replace image, export, delete). See [Floors and plans](/using/floors).
- **+ New plan** adds a floor or a view.
- **Undo / Redo** and the save status (*Saving…* / *✓ All changes saved*). There is no Save button: everything is saved automatically.
- **✓ I'm ready** appears after the first device is placed. See [I'm ready](/using/ready).
- **⋮** on the far right: *Show intro*, *Show/Hide step-by-step guide*, *Choose devices again…*, and the version number (also shown next to the title).

## Steps and guide

The numbered steps **1 Measure · 2 Mark rooms · 3 Place devices** turn into a green ✓ when done. You can click them in any order.

The green box explains the current step and offers *Skip* or *Next*. **Hide guide** turns it off (it is remembered); bring it back with **Show guide** or from the ⋮ menu. Every step opens with the whole plan visible.

## The plan

Controls sit on the plan itself, in the corners:

| Corner | Control |
|---|---|
| Top right | **Layers**: show or hide measurements, rooms and devices. See [Layers](/using/layers). |
| Bottom right | **Zoom**: − / + in 25 % steps, **Fit** for 100 %. (The mouse wheel scrolls the page; it doesn't zoom.) |
| Bottom left | **Scale bar**, once the plan is measured. |

Drag an empty spot to pan when zoomed in. Markers keep their size when you zoom.

## The list

The right column changes with the step: **Measurements** in step 1, **Rooms on this floor** in step 2, **Devices to place** in step 3. On narrow screens it moves below the plan.

## Legend and display options

Under the plan: the colour legend, **Colour by** (Connection, Status or None) and **Names** (on hover, always or off).
