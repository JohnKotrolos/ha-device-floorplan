# Phantom devices

**A device that doesn't exist physically: one combined value from devices anywhere in your home.**

The temperature of a room with two thermometers, "are any windows open", "is anyone moving upstairs": a phantom is that answer, with a spot of its own on the plan.

On the dashboard card a phantom looks like **any other reading or marker**: the card doesn't show where a value comes from.

## When to use it

| Use a phantom | Not a phantom |
|---|---|
| One reading per room from two sensors | Several devices on one spot → [bond](/together/bonds) |
| "Windows": any open | One device in two places → [split device](/together/split) |
| Motion anywhere upstairs | |

## Make one

1. In **3 · Place devices**, press the **phantom button** on the plan (next to Layers). A new, empty phantom appears with its card open.
2. **Drag devices that are already on the plan onto it**: they join and jump straight back to their own spot (they never move).
3. With two or more it's ready; move it wherever the value should show.

![A phantom and its card](/screens/phantom-card.webp){.shot}

The **phantom card**: name, *Combined devices* (✕ takes one out; **Uses: All readings ▾** under a member picks which of its sensors count, e.g. only its room temperature), and **Value on the card** for numbers: average, lowest, highest or one device. Motion is *detected if any*, doors and windows are *open if any* (*2 open*).

## On the card

ACs and thermostats count with the temperature and humidity they report themselves (a humidity of 0 is ignored). An extra marker or chip in every overlay where its values fit (Comfort, Security, Live, custom overlays…); its devices **keep their own markers**. Tap it for a popup with every device's value (and a 24 h graph for readings). No lines are drawn on the card: the popup lists the devices. Show it outside the plan with the [chip card](/reference/chip).

## Examples

- **Child's room, two thermometers** (window and bed): the phantom shows *20.4°* with *💧 71%* under it; the popup shows both with a graph, so you see if the window side gets cold.
- **Windows**: every window contact in one phantom; a chip in the wallpanel's top row shows *All closed* or *2 open*.
- **Motion upstairs**: all motion sensors upstairs, *Detected* if any of them is.
