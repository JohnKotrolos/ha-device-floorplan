# Roadmap

What's coming, roughly in order. No dates: it's built in spare time, and your feedback moves things up. Updated with each release.

## Next 1-2

- **Home Replay**: a scrubber that plays the last 24 hours of your home in Live, in fast motion (lights, doors, cameras, the robot's path). Drag back to any moment and see what happened.
- **New card editor**: settings, a large live preview and your markers side by side, the preview your floorplan deserves.
- **Also show**: more than one overlay at once (e.g. Live and Energy), under Layers.
- **Zigbee2MQTT** network map (roles and links), like ZHA today.
- **Device Floorplan Localisation**: framework and supported languages.

## Later

- **Mobile card - Foundation**: room cards in portrait (only rooms where something happens), the plan in landscape.
- **Mobile card - We Go Mobile**: release for the Mobile Card celebration.
- **Doors and covers**: your own images on the card.

## Done recently

- **0.11 Energy**: today's use per device and room, a day scrubber, flow lines into the electrical panel, solar, battery and sub-meters. Also cameras in Live and Security, card editor sections, Info chips settings, Fix proportions.
- **0.10 Cleaning the house**: robot vacuums on the plan (Xiaomi, Dreame, Valetudo).
